Gaze Lab

See where your eyes actually go

Point your webcam at this screen and look at an advert the way you normally would. We record where you genuinely looked — not what an algorithm guessed — then point you at the attention research that explains that pattern.

Three things worth knowing first

  • Your video never leaves this page. Everything runs on your own computer. No frame, no screenshot, no eye data is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. There is no upload code in the project.
  • Your browser will ask for camera access. That happens only when you click the button below — never on page load. Decline and nothing breaks; you can still use the rest of NeuroInsight, or view a demo session.
  • This is not a brain scanner. A webcam can see where you look. It cannot see what you feel or what your neurons are doing, and this app never pretends otherwise.
