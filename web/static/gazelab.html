This records where you actually looked, then shows you which attention networks the literature links to that pattern of looking. Real measurements of you, real research about the brain.

Before we begin

  • Your video never leaves this browser. Every frame is processed on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, recorded or sent anywhere.
  • We request camera access. Your browser will ask. If you decline, nothing else happens and no video exists.
  • You are a participant, not a subject. Nothing here is medical and nothing is diagnostic.
