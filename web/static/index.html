    What this tool does, and what it cannot do

    NeuroInsight computes properties of images. It does not measure people.

    How findings are labelled

    Measurement
    Computed directly from your pixels. Recomputable and exact.
    Model output
    A prediction from a pretrained algorithm. An informed guess, not a fact.
    Literature
    A mechanism established by published research — true in general, not specific to your image.
    Hypothesis
    Our reading of your image. Plausible, unproven.
    Not determinable
    Cannot be established from a static image. We say so rather than guess.

    What is deliberately absent

    There is no emotion score, no purchase-intent probability, and no neural activation estimate anywhere in this report. Producing those would require participant data and a model trained and validated on that specific outcome. We do not have either, so a number here would be fiction dressed as measurement.

      Not a medical or commercial instrument
